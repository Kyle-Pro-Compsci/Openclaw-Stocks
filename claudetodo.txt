[Log entries are claims that can only be settled by waiting. "Semis should outperform today." "This move looks unsustainable." No chart of the past resolves those; only the future does.

But the rule breaks on my own schema. I included likely_cause in the history entry — "moved because of the order announcement." That's a causal claim, and a chart can't verify causation. By my rule it would have to go in the logs, which is clearly wrong; it's exactly the kind of thing you want attached to the day.

The better rule is settled vs. pending:

Settled — about a day that has already happened, and won't change with time → history. Includes interpretation, like why it moved and what happened at the level. It's a reading of a closed event.
Pending — awaiting resolution, only gradeable by what happens next → logs. Views, theses, predictions.
That's the property that actually matters, and "chart-verifiable" was just a proxy for it that leaks on causal claims.]
